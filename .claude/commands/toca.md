---
description: Install the Toca Boca style world (bedroom + character creator) into the open Studio place
---
Connect to Roblox Studio (see CLAUDE.md). Then read `toca-world/InstallTocaWorld.lua` and run its full contents with `execute_luau` (timeoutSeconds 120). Check `get_output_log` for errors and fix them in `toca-world/src/`, then re-run `python3 toca-world/build.py` and the installer. Take a screenshot of the bedroom, then tell the user in two or three lines what was built and to press **Play** and tap the ✨ button.
